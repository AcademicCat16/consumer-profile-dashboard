Consumer Lab Privacy Policy

Covers the Consumer Lab dashboard (academiccat16.github.io/consumer-profile-dashboard) and the Consumer Lab Connector Chrome extension. Effective 30 October 2026.

Summary

  • Your browsing history is read only after you approve it, and is analysed only on your own device, in the dashboard tab.
  • We have no server. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share your data, and we do not send it to AI services, analytics or advertisers.
  • You can disconnect at any time. Closing the dashboard tab erases the loaded data, unless you chose to keep it on your device.

The extension never requests or reads cookies, passwords, form data, authentication tokens, payment information or browser credential databases. It does not run on pages in the background and does not change any website.

How the dashboard uses data

The dashboard is a static web page. It parses your history, or files you import, in your browser to show counts, visualizations, descriptive shopping tendencies, segments and rankings of products you captured. Records about health, politics, religion, sexuality and dating are excluded before analysis. The profile is descriptive. It is not a clinical or validated psychological measurement.

By default, data is held only in the open tab's memory. If you switch on Keep my data on this device, it is saved in your browser's IndexedDB on that device only, until you switch it off or delete it.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell, transfer or disclose your data to anyone. We do not use it for advertising, creditworthiness or lending decisions, or any purpose unrelated to showing you your own profile. We cannot see your data: it never reaches us.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third-party requests

The dashboard loads web fonts from Google Fonts, which receives a standard request (your IP address and browser details) but none of your history. Product images you captured load from the retailer that published them.

Your controls

  • Disconnect: Data & Privacy → Disconnect Chrome, or the extension menu → Disconnect. Either removes Chrome's history permission and the dashboard's access.
  • Delete: Data & Privacy lets you delete single records, whole sources, or everything, including any copy kept on your device.
  • Uninstall: removing the extension at chrome://extensions deletes its local storage.
