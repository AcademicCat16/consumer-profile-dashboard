Connect your Chrome history?

this dashboard wants to read your own Chrome history to build your consumer profile.

  • Reads Visited page addresses, page titles and visit times from the last 90 days
  • Stays local The data goes only to that dashboard tab, where it is analysed. It is never uploaded or sent to AI services.
  • Never reads Cookies, passwords, form data or sign-in details
  • You control it Disconnect at any time from the dashboard, this extension's menu, or chrome://extensions

Next, Chrome will ask you to confirm “Read your browsing history”. This happens once.
